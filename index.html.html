A Title

🔎 Investigate conditions for the lamp to turn on and connect observations with current electricity.

B Objectives

  • Investigate conditions required for the lamp to work.
  • Connect observation results with the concept of electric current.

C Tools & Materials

  • Battery
  • Small light bulb
  • Connecting cable
  • Switch (if available)
  • Alternative: simulation of series circuit

D Activity Steps

  1. Connect battery, cables, and light bulb into a complete (closed) circuit.
  2. Observe the light bulb.
  3. Disconnect one cable, then observe the light bulb again.
  4. Record the difference in observations in the table below.
⚠️ Always follow the teacher's safety instructions while using the equipment.
